## Who We're Selling To

We're not trying to sell to everyone. We're going deep into a few industries where the pain is obvious and the budget is there.

### Real Estate — Start Here

This is our best first market. Real estate agents in Hyderabad, Bangalore, and Mumbai are drowning in leads from 99acres, MagicBricks, and Housing.com. They get 200 leads a day and can't call all of them fast enough. By the time they call, the lead has already talked to three competitors.

Our AI calls within 60 seconds of a lead coming in. It speaks in Telugu or Hindi, qualifies the lead, and books a site visit. The agent only talks to people who are actually interested.

A real estate developer spending ₹2 lakhs a month on lead generation will pay ₹15,000 a month for this without thinking twice. That's less than 10% of their ad spend, and it makes the other 90% more effective.

Where to find them: Real estate Facebook groups, LinkedIn, property expos, and honestly just walking into any real estate office in Hyderabad and asking to speak to the sales head.

### EdTech and Coaching Institutes

Every coaching institute has the same problem. Students and parents fill out enquiry forms, and then the counsellors spend their entire day making calls. Most of those calls go unanswered. The ones that do answer often aren't serious. By the time a counsellor reaches a serious lead, it's been 48 hours and the student has already enrolled somewhere else.

Our AI calls every enquiry within minutes, figures out which course they're interested in, what their budget is, and whether they're ready to enroll. The counsellor only talks to the ones who are ready.

Price point: ₹10,000–₹20,000 a month. For an institute doing ₹50 lakhs a year in revenue, this is nothing.

### Insurance Agencies

Insurance leads from aggregators like PolicyBazaar are expensive and time-sensitive. If you don't call within 5 minutes, the lead goes cold or gets picked up by a competitor. Most agencies can't staff enough people to call that fast.

Our AI calls instantly, collects the basic details, and books a callback with a human agent for the serious ones. The agent's time is spent only on qualified prospects.

### Hospitals and Clinics

Appointment reminders, follow-up calls after procedures, test result notifications — all of this is currently done manually by staff who have better things to do. Our AI handles all of it automatically, in the patient's preferred language.

### Car Dealerships

Test drive enquiries, service reminders, follow-ups on people who visited the showroom but didn't buy. All of this is currently falling through the cracks. Our AI catches it.

### Recruitment Agencies

Screening 200 candidates before a human interview is exhausting. Our AI does the initial screening call, scores each candidate, and only sends the qualified ones to the recruiter.

## How to Find the First Clients

**LinkedIn** is the fastest. Search for "Real Estate Developer Hyderabad" or "Coaching Institute Owner Bangalore." Send a short message. Don't pitch immediately — ask if they have a lead follow-up problem. They always do.

**WhatsApp groups** are underrated. Real estate agent groups, business owner groups, HR professional groups. Drop a short message with a demo video link.

**Walk in.** Seriously. Walk into a real estate office, ask for the sales manager, and say "I have an AI that calls your leads in 60 seconds and speaks Telugu. Can I show you a 5-minute demo?" You'll get meetings.

**Referrals** are the best long-term channel. Give the first client one month free if they refer two clients. Word spreads fast in tight-knit industries like real estate.

**Digital marketing agencies** are a hidden channel. They already have clients who need this. Partner with two or three agencies and let them resell it.

## The Sales Conversation

When you reach out, keep it short. Something like this works well:

*"Hi [Name], I noticed you're in real estate. We built an AI that calls your leads within 60 seconds, qualifies them in Telugu and English, and sends you only the ones ready to buy. Your team only talks to serious buyers. Can I show you a 5-minute demo?"*

That's it. Don't explain the technology. Don't talk about FastAPI or Whisper or Groq. Talk about the problem they have and the result they'll get.

**The demo should take 15 minutes:**

Start by showing the dashboard — it looks professional and clean, which builds trust immediately. Add a test lead and run the AI call simulator. Let them hear the AI speak in Telugu. That's usually the moment they lean forward. Show them the lead score and the analysis. Then show the pricing. Then ask: "Want to start with the free trial?"

**When they push back:**

If they say the AI doesn't sound human enough, play them the Telugu female voice sample. It's genuinely good.

If they say their leads need a personal touch, explain that the AI qualifies and the human closes. Their team only talks to people who are already interested.

If they say it's too expensive, remind them they're spending ₹20,000 on a newspaper ad that runs for one day. This runs 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, for a month.

If they say let me think about it, offer the free trial. Zero risk. Let the product sell itself.
